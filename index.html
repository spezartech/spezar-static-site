Awards

TNSI Award from EDII TN, Government of TN

Proud to have been recognized as one of the top student startups in Tamil Nadu and to have received the prestigious TNSI Award from the Government of Tamil Nadu, Entrepreneurship Development and Innovation Institute along with a seed grant of Rs. 1 Lakh rupees. Arun Roy, IAS - Government of India, Secretary of MSME, Director of EDII, the Mayor of Tambaram Corporation, and members of the Legislative Assembly.
